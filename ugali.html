Ugali recipe

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • Maize flour
  • Water

Steps

  1. Boil water in a pot.
  2. Gradually add maize flour to the boiling water while stirring continuously to avoid lumps.
  3. Continue stirring until the mixture thickens and pulls away from the sides of the pot.
  4. Remove from heat and let it sit for a few minutes.
  5. Shape the ugali into a round form using a wooden spoon or your hands.
  6. Serve hot with your favorite stew or vegetables.
