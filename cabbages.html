Cabbage recipe

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cabbage, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: 1 tomato, chopped

Steps

  1. Heat the cooking oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the sliced onion and minced garlic to the pan. Sauté until the onion becomes translucent.
  3. If using, add the chopped tomato and cook for another 2-3 minutes until it softens.
  4. Add the chopped cabbage to the pan. Stir well to combine with the sautéed onions and garlic.
  5. Cook the cabbage for about 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it becomes tender but still slightly crisp.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Adjust seasoning as needed.
  7. Remove from heat and serve hot as a side dish or main course.
