Fried eggs recipe

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • cooking fat
  • salt

Steps

Follow these steps to make delicious fried eggs:

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat them lightly with a fork. Add a pinch of salt to taste.
  2. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and add a small amount of cooking fat (butter or oil).
  3. Once the fat is hot, pour the beaten eggs into the pan.
  4. Let the eggs cook undisturbed for a few seconds until they start to set around the edges.
  5. Using a spatula, gently stir the eggs from the edges towards the center, allowing uncooked egg to flow to the edges.
  6. Continue cooking and stirring until the eggs are mostly set but still slightly runny. Remove from heat as they will continue to cook from residual heat.
  7. Serve immediately, optionally garnished with fresh herbs or grated cheese.
